Full methodology
01

Source

Official Census State × Sector Business Dynamics Statistics, with source checksum recorded in the repository.

02

Suppressions

Disclosure/suppression codes are converted to missing values — never silently treated as zero.

03

Comparable ranks

Structural inputs are percentile-ranked within each year and sector, preventing cross-industry scale differences from dominating.

04

Prediction protocol

Features at year t are used only to predict an outcome at t+2; train, validation, and holdout periods are separated.

05

Baselines

Complex models must compete against a simple persistence baseline; improvement is reported rather than assumed.

06

Failure analysis

Sector-level errors and weak performance are shown so users can see where the model is least reliable.

Research integrity

What this release does not prove

No M&A causality yet

BDS measures business dynamics, not acquisitions. Transaction-level validation must be added before any claim that the model predicts consolidation events.

Fragmentation is a proxy

Firms per 1,000 employees is useful for structural comparison but does not equal a market-share concentration measure such as HHI.

Public-data publication lags

The latest BDS release currently covers through 2023. The platform timestamps data vintages so later releases can be replayed honestly.

Not investment advice

The score does not evaluate price, leverage, cash flow, management quality, deal terms, or expected returns.

Immutable forecast ledger

No M&A forecast has been issued yet.

That is deliberate. The repository contains the forecast protocol and schema, but a future consolidation forecast will only be frozen and timestamped after a sufficiently verified transaction-outcome corpus passes the research milestone gate.
